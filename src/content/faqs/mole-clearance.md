question: "Why do I need a dermatologist before mole removal?"
answer: "Radiofrequency destroys the tissue as it removes it, so nothing is left to send to pathology. A mole removed cosmetically is a mole that can never be tested — and if it was a melanoma, that diagnosis is simply lost. We require written confirmation that a lesion is benign before we touch it, and we will refer you if you do not have one."
