question: "Is laser safe for Indian skin tones?"
answer: "Yes, with the right wavelength — and that is the whole issue. Most of our clients are Fitzpatrick IV or V, where epidermal melanin competes with the hair for the laser's energy. We open on a long-pulse Nd:YAG 1064nm, which passes through that melanin safely, and move to the diode only once hair has thinned. Machines tuned for pale skin are how deeper tones end up with burns and pale patches."
