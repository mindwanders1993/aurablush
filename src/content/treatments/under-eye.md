Almost everyone who books this treatment believes dark circles are one condition. They are at least four, they often coexist, and treating the wrong one is why so many people have spent money on this and seen nothing.

**Pigmentation** — genuine melanin deposition, common in deeper skin tones and often genetic — reads brown and does not change when you stretch the skin. **Vascular shadowing** is the bluish cast of blood vessels showing through skin barely half a millimetre thick; it worsens with fatigue, allergies and nasal congestion. **Thin or crepey skin** simply reveals whatever is underneath it. And **hollowing** at the tear trough is not colour at all — it is a shadow cast by lost volume, which is why brightening creams do nothing for it.

The first appointment is diagnosis. We stretch the skin, change the lighting angle, and ask about sleep, allergies, iron levels and family history. Then we match the protocol: gentle periorbital-safe acid work and tyrosinase inhibitors for pigment; barrier and circulation support for the vascular type; regenerative skin-quality treatment for thin, crepey skin.

**Where we stop is as important as where we start.** If the dominant problem is hollowing, no amount of surface work will fix it — that needs volume, and we will refer you to a medical injector rather than sell you three sessions that cannot work. Pigment-targeting lasers in the periorbital area require corneal shields and a very experienced operator; we do not offer them here, and we would rather say so than improvise near an eye.

Some of the biggest gains in this area are not clinical at all. Chronic allergies, poor sleep and low iron all show up under the eyes first, and clients who fix those alongside a course consistently do better than those who do not.
