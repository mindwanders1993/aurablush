Pigmentation is the slowest thing on a face to shift and the easiest to make worse. The mechanism is simple — melanocytes overproduce melanin in response to UV, heat, inflammation or hormones — but the treatment order matters enormously, and getting it wrong causes post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation that is darker than what you started with.

So we follow the evidence ladder, in order. **Topicals and photoprotection first**: tranexamic acid, azelaic acid, kojic acid or arbutin, all of which interrupt melanin production at the tyrosinase step, plus rigorous daily SPF. **Chemical peels second**, at a depth chosen for your skin type, to clear pigment that has already reached the epidermis. Devices are a third-line option, not an opening move, and we will say so even when you have come in asking for laser.

That order exists because of one finding that runs through the literature: lasers clear pigment faster than peels, and cause considerably more post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation doing it — particularly in Fitzpatrick IV to VI skin, which is most of Varanasi. Faster is not better if the rebound is darker than what you walked in with. This is why we turn down laser requests for melasma more often than we accept them.

**Melasma specifically is managed, not cured.** It is hormonal and it recurs, and any clinic promising permanent clearance is selling you something. What a course achieves is a real reduction in density and a maintenance routine that keeps it there — and recurrence is almost always a UV story.

Which is the hard part in this city. Varanasi gets strong UV nearly year-round, and a summer afternoon here will undo weeks of careful work in a single outing. Sunscreen is not a footnote to this treatment; it *is* the treatment, and the clinic work is what accelerates it. If you are not willing to wear SPF 50 daily and reapply it, tell us at consultation and we will save you the money.

Depth determines everything. Epidermal pigment responds well to topicals and peels; dermal and mixed pigment responds partially at best. We assess which you have at consultation and tell you honestly what is achievable before you commit to a course.
