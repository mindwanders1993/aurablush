Lip blushing deposits a soft, diffused wash of pigment through the body of the lip rather than drawing a line around it. The point is not to make you look like you are wearing lipstick — it is to give back the definition and colour that lips lose with age, and to even out a border that has gone soft.

We custom-mix pigment against your natural lip tone and your skin's undertone. Lips with a strong blue or purple cast need neutralising first, which is a technique in its own right; skipping it is why some lip blush heals grey. If that is your starting point we will tell you at consultation and plan for it.

**Cold sores are the one thing that can ruin this treatment.** The trauma of the needle reactivates dormant herpes simplex in a large majority of people who carry it, and a cold sore during healing takes the pigment with it when it scabs. If you have ever had one — even once, even years ago — you need a course of antivirals starting three days before. We will not treat without it.

Compared with filler, lip blush changes colour and perceived shape but adds no volume. Many clients do both; if you do, have the filler first and leave four weeks between.
