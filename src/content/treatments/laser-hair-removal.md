Laser hair removal works by selective photothermolysis: the laser targets melanin in the hair shaft, heat travels down to the follicle, and the follicle's ability to produce hair is disabled. The critical consequence is that it only works on hair that is actively growing — and at any given moment only a fraction of your hair is in that phase. That, not the equipment, is why a course is unavoidable.

Most of our clients are Fitzpatrick IV or V, and that single fact decides the machine. Melanin in the epidermis competes with melanin in the hair for the laser's energy, so a wavelength tuned for pale European skin dumps heat into the skin itself — which is how deeper skin tones end up with burns and pale patches at cheap clinics. **We open on a long-pulse 1064nm Nd:YAG**, which passes through epidermal melanin and reaches the follicle, and move to an 810nm diode with contact cooling once the hair has thinned and lightened. Published work supports the diode at low fluence and high repetition rate into types IV to VI; it is the second half of the course here, not the first.

**Expect a course, and expect it to be spaced by area.** Face and neck at four weeks; underarms and bikini at four to five; legs and arms at five to six; back and chest at six to eight, because growth cycles differ across the body. Plan on eight to ten sessions — that is the honest number for Indian skin, where more of the laser's energy is absorbed before it reaches the follicle. Anyone quoting you six for a full course either has a different machine in mind or a different skin type. Published work puts average reduction at around 61% after just three sessions, which is a useful marker of whether your course is on track.

**The patch test is not a formality, and it is a separate visit.** We place a small test area 24 to 48 hours ahead and read the response before choosing your parameters. Tanned skin, photosensitising medication and unexpected reactions all get caught here rather than on your arm mid-session. We do not skip it for anyone, including clients treated elsewhere — so if you are visiting Varanasi, budget two appointments at least a day apart, and tell us at booking how long you are in the city.

The tan question is not theoretical here. A few days walking the ghats or an afternoon on a boat is enough sun to change your settings, and June temperatures make it worse. If you have been out in it, we test lower and treat lower, or we reschedule.

One thing to be clear about before you book: this is *reduction*, not removal. A full course leaves most people with 80 to 90% less hair, and what returns is finer and lighter. Hormonal areas — the face and jawline especially — usually need a maintenance session once or twice a year, and PCOS is common enough among our clients that we plan for it from the first consultation rather than treating it as a surprise at session six.
