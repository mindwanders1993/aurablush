Microneedling — properly, collagen induction therapy — passes a cartridge of very fine needles through the skin at a controlled depth, creating thousands of microscopic channels. Each one is a small, deliberate wound. Your skin responds the way it responds to any wound: it floods the area with growth factors and lays down new collagen and elastin. The treatment does not add anything to your face. It provokes your face into rebuilding itself.

Depth is the whole craft. Fine lines and pores need a shallow pass; rolling acne scars need to go deep enough to break the fibrous tethers holding the scar down. We adjust depth by region across a single face — thinner around the eyes and forehead, deeper on the cheeks.

**This is a course, not a treatment.** One session gives you a glow that is mostly swelling. The evidence sits with three to six sessions spaced four to six weeks apart, because that is roughly how long a collagen remodelling cycle takes. Texture change starts showing around week four; the full result of a course lands at three to six months and keeps improving after your last appointment.

The trade-off is honest downtime. You will be red like a moderate sunburn for 24 to 72 hours, tight for a day, and possibly flaky around day three. Book it a week clear of anything you need to look photographed for.
