Microblading is a brow embroidery technique in which a fine blade of clustered needles deposits pigment in short, hair-like strokes just beneath the surface of the skin. It is not a tattoo gun and it does not go as deep as body tattooing, which is why it softens and fades rather than spreading and greying.

Everything happens before any pigment is opened. We map the brow with calipers against your own bone structure and natural asymmetry, then match a pigment to your undertone — cool, warm or neutral — because the wrong undertone is what turns a healed brow orange or ash. You see the drawn shape and agree to it first.

**The perfecting visit is not optional.** Brows heal 20–30% lighter than they look on the day, and roughly one stroke in five will not take. The visit at 6–8 weeks is where the set is finished, and it is included in the price.

If your skin is very oily, say so at consultation. Fine strokes blur into a solid shape on oily skin within a year, and we would rather put you in powder or combination brows than sell you a result that will not hold.
