# Founder intake — questions for the website

Everything below fills a real gap on the site. Anything not answered stays a
visible `[PLACEHOLDER]` rather than something invented.

Answers can be rough — bullet points, voice notes, Hindi or English. Writing
them well is the website's job, not hers.

---

## 1. Name and title

1. Full name as she wants it published.
2. What should the title say — "Founder & Artist", "Founder & PMU Artist",
   something else?
3. Is "Aura Blush" the final studio name, or a working title?

## 2. Training and certificates

*Send scans or photos of each certificate — the exact wording on them matters,
because that is what can legally be claimed on the site.*

4. Which courses/academies, and what year for each?
5. Any certificate that names a specific technique (microblading, lip blush,
   microneedling, laser safety, RF)?
6. Trained under anyone notable, or in another city or country?
7. Any licence or registration number that should appear?

## 3. Experience

8. How long has she been doing this, and roughly how many clients?
9. Was she working somewhere else before opening her own studio?
10. Which treatment is she strongest at — the one she'd want to be known for?
11. Anything on the current menu she has **not** trained for yet? (Important —
    see section 6.)

## 4. Her story — a few sentences each

> The artist page currently carries a **draft bio written on her behalf**, based
> only on what we know: three years, certificate-trained, solo, Sigra. It is
> plausible but it is not her voice. These answers replace it.

12. How did she get into this work?
13. What does she do differently from other studios in Varanasi?
14. Is there a client result she is proudest of?
15. Something she refuses to do, even when asked?

## 5. The studio

16. **Studio address** — locality is confirmed as **Sigra**. Still needed: the
    building/shop number, street name and PIN code. The site currently shows
    "Sigra, Varanasi" and says the full address is to follow.
16a. The landmark locals would actually use for directions — that matters more
    than the PIN code here.
17. Real phone / WhatsApp number, and the email to publish.
18. Actual opening days and hours.
19. Is the studio open yet, or is there a launch date?
20. Home visits or studio only?

## 6. Scope — the ones that matter legally

21. **Laser** — does she own or have access to a laser machine, and which one?
    Is she laser-safety certified? (Nd:YAG matters for Indian skin tones.)
22. **Mole removal** — is she trained for this, and does she have a
    dermatologist she works with who can clear lesions in writing?
23. Any treatment on the site she would rather not offer at launch?

> If the answer to 21 or 22 is "not yet", that is completely fine — those two
> services come off the site until they are real. It is much safer to launch
> with eight treatments she can back than ten she cannot.

## 7. Pricing

Current prices on the site are **researched estimates**, not her numbers.

24. Her actual price for each treatment.
25. Does she offer course/package pricing?
26. Is the price inclusive of GST — and is she GST-registered yet?
27. Does she charge for consultations?

## 8. Photos

28. A portrait of her for the artist page.
29. Photos of the studio once it is set up.
30. **Her own before/after work**, if she has any, with the client's permission.

> The treatment photos currently on the site are licensed stock. They are fine
> as placeholders, but her own healed work will convert far better — and stock
> photos must never be captioned as her results.

## 9. Reviews

The eight reviews on the site are **written placeholders and must come off
before launch.** Publishing invented reviews is illegal advertising in most
markets and would be an awful start for a new business.

31. Any real clients — even from before the studio — willing to give a
    testimonial with their name?
32. Is there a Google Business Profile or Instagram where real reviews already
    exist?
